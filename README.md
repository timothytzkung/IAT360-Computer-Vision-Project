## Overview
Our computer vision project idea is to identify car part damage and categorize the type of damage, such as scratches or flat tires. The problem that we are addressing is the timely inspection process to manually assess car damage, especially in large volumes. Our model will use Object Detection under Supervised Learning to automate this process, potentially improving speed, consistency and cost-efficiency. Streamlining this process can improve use cases in real-world applications, such as insurance claim processing, repair cost estimations, and vehicle inspections. For instance,  insurance companies such as BCAA and ICBC can expect faster return times on insurance claims, as well as repair shops can efficiently detect car damage to figure out cost estimations for their customers. Additionally, car dealerships can utilize this model to assess vehicle trade-in value in minor damage use cases, such as scratches or dents.
